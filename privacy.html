Privacy

Your privacy

This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it does not track you.

Fonts

Pages load the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s font service when a page opens.

The documentation library

Documents in the library are stored encrypted. Your passphrase is used only inside your own browser to unlock them. It is never sent to this site, to GitHub or to anyone else.

Once unlocked, documents are decrypted on your device only for as long as the library stays open. The library locks itself after 15 minutes without use.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as described in its own privacy statement.
