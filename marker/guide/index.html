1. Choose how to play

The Marker is a companion to The Scorecard and a complete standalone scorecard for one to four players.

1. Choose how to play: steps

  1. Choose Sign in with The Scorecard to use your existing account and pick golfers from your groups. Eligible finished rounds can be posted for those golfers.
  2. Choose Play without an account to type player names, keep the card on this device and share it. No Scorecard account is required.
  3. Continue your round appears when a round is already saved. A saved correction or re-post instead shows Continue your correction or Continue your re-post. Open the web app while connected before leaving for the course.

2. Sign in and recover access

Use the same email address and password as The Scorecard.

2. Sign in and recover access: steps

  1. Enter your Scorecard email and password, then tap Sign in. On the start screen, tap Start a new round.
  2. For a forgotten password, enter your email and tap Forgot the password? Send a reset email. Follow the email to reset your shared Scorecard password.
  3. Signing in needs an internet connection. If you have no account, return and choose Play without an account. Account and group setup are handled in The Scorecard.

3. Set up the round

Check these choices before you tee off.

3. Set up the round: steps

  1. Tap the date to open the calendar. Select the month, year and day played, or type the date as year, month, day. Future dates are blocked. Choose the course and the tee actually used.
  2. Choose 18 holes, Front 9 or Back 9. A nine-hole card can be shared but cannot be posted to The Scorecard.
  3. Set the Starting hole for a shotgun start. An 18-hole round starting on 10 goes through 18, then 1 through 9.
  4. Choose your score-entry layout. The selected course and tee apply to everyone on this card; use separate cards for different tees.

4. Choose the round date

Use the app’s calendar to select a past date or today.

4. Choose the round date: steps

  1. Tap the date in Set up the round to open the calendar. Use the arrows for the previous or next month. Tap the month name or year to choose it directly.
  2. Tap the day you played. The calendar closes with that date selected. Future days cannot be selected.
  3. For another date, use Or type it: enter year, month, day, for example 2025 05 18 or 20250518, then tap Use what I typed. Invalid or future dates show an error.
  4. Today selects today. Use this date closes the calendar keeping the selected date. Changing the date clears the chosen game; check Part of a game again.

5. Find and check a course

Check the course data against the club’s current scorecard.

5. Find and check a course: steps

  1. Tap Choose a course. Enter a name and choose Show saved or Search online. Select the right club and tee.
  2. Back in setup, tap Check the holes (par, yards, handicap) when available. Compare the hole details with the club’s card.
  3. If details are missing or wrong, use the correction option or Enter a course yourself. Online search needs a connection; previously saved courses are available locally.
  4. Withdrawn course details cannot be used. Follow the message to find corrected data or enter the current club card. Shared course administration belongs in The Scorecard. If a locally saved course has a Delete button, it removes that course from this device only, after confirmation. Shared-library entries do not show this button.

6. Enter a course yourself

Manual entry is available even when you cannot read a photo.

6. Enter a course yourself: steps

  1. Choose Enter a course yourself. Type the course name and tee. Copy rating and slope from the club card if you intend to post.
  2. Check every hole’s par: the form starts with par 4, so change it where needed. Yards are optional.
  3. Enter each HCP / Handicap / S.I. number exactly as printed. Use each number from 1 to 18 once, or leave all blank. Missing HCP makes stroke allocation approximate.
  4. Tap Use this course. Manually entered data can be used for eligible Scorecard rounds; a photo or provider verification is not required.

7. Read the club card from a photo

This reads course details, not the players’ handwritten scores.

7. Read the club card from a photo: steps

  1. Open Enter a course yourself. Type the course name and use a photo button. Lay the club card flat in good light.
  2. Include all 18 holes, every tee, ratings, slopes, par, handicap numbers and printed totals. Avoid glare and cut-off edges.
  3. Wait for the reading spinner. If asked, take a closer picture of the missing or unclear half.
  4. Photo reading needs internet and an available daily allowance. Check the displayed pictures-left message. If unavailable, enter the details by hand.

8. Confirm the photo reading

A green check means the app’s checks passed. You must still compare the values with the printed card.

8. Confirm the photo reading: steps

  1. Review both nines and every tee, not just the tee you will play. Check rating, slope, yards, par, handicap numbers and totals.
  2. Red marks indicate missing or problematic values. Retake the requested part, or choose Correct it by hand instead and select a tee.
  3. Read any warning about the printed card’s own totals. Confirm only when the displayed data matches the card.
  4. Tap This matches the card when available. Signed-in, verified data becomes shared course data; without an account it is saved on this device. Then choose your tee.

9. Choose one to four players

Group golfers can be posted; typed guests are scored and shared only.

9. Choose one to four players: steps

  1. Tap Choose the players. Signed-in users select a group and tap existing golfers. You may select golfers from your own groups, up to four total.
  2. Add other people by typing a name and tapping Add. Without an account, enter each player this way. Typed names are guests, even if they resemble a member’s name.
  3. Tap a selected member again to deselect; use Remove for a typed player. Check the names, then tap Tee off on hole ….

10. Attach an available game

Game selection is optional and appears during setup for signed-in users when games are available.

10. Attach an available game: steps

  1. Set the correct date first. Under Part of a game, choose the appropriate listed game or leave Not part of a game selected.
  2. Only available games covering that date are listed. Group access affects which games you can see.
  3. For a card containing golfers from different groups, the game attaches only to rounds in the game’s group. Create and manage games in The Scorecard.

11. Enter scores, one golfer at a time

Enter the strokes actually taken. The app calculates its handicap adjustment separately.

11. Enter scores, one golfer at a time: steps

  1. Tap the golfer’s name, then tap their score tile. The entered value is highlighted. Tap another value to correct it.
  2. Tap the next golfer’s name and enter that score. Use Type a score for a whole number from 1 to 20.
  3. Use Picked up when the player did not finish the hole. This records X; it is not a zero.
  4. When everyone has an entry, tap the next-hole button. After the final hole, tap Finish. Watch for save errors before continuing.

12. Enter all players together

Switch layouts at any time without losing scores.

12. Enter all players together: steps

  1. Choose All four together in setup, or use Menu to switch during play. It also works with fewer than four players.
  2. Tap + on an empty score to start at par. Then use minus or plus, or tap the score field and type a whole number from 1 to 20.
  3. Type X for picked up. If your keyboard does not offer X, switch to one-golfer mode and tap Picked up.
  4. Enter a score for everyone, then advance. Blank fields remain unfinished; they do not count as par.

13. Fix scores before posting

Return to the totals directly after changing a hole.

13. Fix scores before posting: steps

  1. On Round finished, tap Fix a score, or open Review card. Tap the row for the hole you want to change.
  2. Choose the golfer and change their score in either scoring layout. Tap Done fixing — back to the totals when the card is complete.
  3. Check the totals again, then post eligible rounds or keep/share the card. You no longer need to walk through the remaining holes after fixing a completed card.

14. Understand the totals

Illustrative 18-hole example: gross 90, adjusted 88 and course handicap 10.

14. Understand the totals: steps

  1. Gross adds entered strokes. For an X, the app substitutes that hole’s handicap limit, so it is not a count of strokes actually finished on that hole.
  2. Adjusted caps high hole scores at the app’s handicap limit: normally par + 2 + strokes received. With no known course handicap, the app uses par + 5.
  3. Net is gross minus course handicap when the round is complete and a handicap is available. Do not use a nine-hole net display as a posted 18-hole result.
  4. E means even with par. + and − show over and under par. Stroke notes depend on the selected tee, the player’s index and hole handicap data.

15. Post eligible rounds to Scorecard

Posting and sharing are different actions.

15. Post eligible rounds to Scorecard: steps

  1. Check all totals. Tap Post … rounds to The Scorecard when shown. The number counts eligible group golfers, not guests.
  2. Posting requires a signed-in card, at least one selected group golfer, 18 holes, rating and slope, and a score on every required hole. The server checks the submission too.
  3. Nine-hole cards, standalone cards and typed guests are kept and shared instead. Read the reason if no Post button is available.
  4. A standalone card does not become postable just by signing in later. Start in signed-in mode and select the actual group golfers when posting is your goal.

16. Confirm the posting result

Do not treat Waiting or Checking as a successful post.

16. Confirm the posting result: steps

  1. Waiting means the card is saved locally for sending. Reopen The Marker on the same browser, sign in to the original account if needed and leave it open with a connection.
  2. Checking means the submission reached the service and is being processed. Allow a few minutes and check again.
  3. Posted ✓ confirms acceptance. Open The Scorecard to verify the rounds. The service recalculates the posting data, so its final values are authoritative.
  4. For Not posted, read the reason and keep or share the card. Do not re-enter the same rounds while the original submission is still waiting or being checked.

17. Correct a posted card

The Marker can now correct hole scores on an eligible posted card. A connection is required.

17. Correct a posted card: steps

  1. Sign in and open Your cards → Correct a posted card, then select a card. You can also open a posted card saved on this device and tap Correct this card. Finish or end another active round first.
  2. The original scorer can correct the card for 24 hours after posting. An owner or admin of every group represented on the card can correct it at any time. The service checks your access.
  3. Tap Fix a score, tap the hole’s row, choose the golfer and change the strokes. Tap Done fixing — back to the totals to return directly. Repeat for other errors.
  4. Review the totals and tap Save the correction. Wait for Corrected in The Scorecard. The affected rounds and handicap indexes are updated together. If no score changed, the app says nothing was saved.
  5. A correction changes hole scores only. It keeps the original players, date, course, tee and game. If someone has changed a round directly in Scorecard, read the refusal and ask an owner/admin to resolve it there.
  6. Cancel the correction discards the current correction after confirmation. Menu offers the same cancel action while correcting. If the connection fails, keep the screen and try again online.

18. Re-post after rounds were deleted

Use Re-post only for the cards the app identifies under Deleted in The Scorecard.

18. Re-post after rounds were deleted: steps

  1. Open Your cards. Under Deleted in The Scorecard, the app names the golfers whose rounds were deleted. Tap Re-post. Opening a saved original card may instead offer Edit and re-post.
  2. The scorer, or an owner/admin of every group on the original card, may re-post. Finish or end another active round first. Only golfers with deleted rounds are put on the new card; surviving rounds are kept.
  3. Use Fix a score, select the hole, change the strokes, then tap Done fixing — back to the totals. Check the date, golfers and totals shown.
  4. Tap Post … rounds to The Scorecard. This creates a new card for the deleted rounds. Wait for Posted ✓. Once accepted, the new card replaces the original copy in this device’s card history.
  5. Hide takes the deleted-round reminder off that list after confirmation; it changes no Scorecard round. If the original card remains on this device, opening it still offers Edit and re-post.
  6. Cancel the re-post discards the current re-post without sending it. If the service says a round still exists or the card was already re-posted, check the current records before proceeding.

19. Share a card

Share during play, after finishing or from Your cards.

19. Share a card: steps

  1. Tap Share the card. Your device may show its native share sheet; otherwise choose WhatsApp, Email, Text message or Copy.
  2. Choose the recipients in your messaging app and send when ready. Sharing sends a text summary with hole scores; it does not post rounds to The Scorecard.
  3. If Copy is blocked, press and hold the displayed text and copy it manually. An unfinished card can also be shared; missing holes appear as dashes.

20. Resume, keep and delete cards

Local cards belong to this device and browser.

20. Resume, keep and delete cards: steps

  1. Return to the start screen through Menu. Use Continue your round to resume. To stop permanently without posting, choose End this round without posting and confirm.
  2. Your cards shows cards saved on this device (up to 30), and signed-in users can also see eligible cards whose rounds were deleted in Scorecard. Open a local card to view, share or use its available correction action.
  3. Delete this card is available only when allowed. Deletion is permanent; posted cards and cards being checked cannot be deleted here.
  4. Clearing browser data or changing devices does not carry local cards across. Share important cards before clearing data or exceeding the history limit.

21. When something goes wrong

Keep the card and resolve the displayed message first.

21. When something goes wrong: steps

  1. No signal: a loaded round can save scores locally. Search, sign-in, photo reading and posting need internet. This web release does not guarantee a fresh offline launch; keep the loaded page available.
  2. Save error: do not assume the latest score was kept. Check browser storage, avoid private browsing and retry. Share a copy before clearing anything.
  3. Missing golfer: check the group and connection. A typed guest cannot be posted as that group golfer. Manage membership in The Scorecard.
  4. Photo or posting problem: read the message, use manual course entry when needed, and check posting status online. For help, use Support on the app’s start screen.

22. The Menu bubble during play

The Menu control appears at the top of the hole screen beside the saved indicator. Tap it without leaving your round.

22. The Menu bubble during play: steps

  1. Tap Menu at the top right while entering scores.
  2. Choose Switch to change the score-entry view. Your entered scores stay saved.
  3. See the whole card lets you review holes; Share the card sends a score summary.
  4. Go to the start screen keeps the active round for Continue your round. End this round without posting is different: it ends the round and keeps a historical card without posting.
  5. While correcting a posted card, Cancel the correction replaces End this round without posting. It abandons the correction after confirmation. Tap Back to hole to return to scoring.
