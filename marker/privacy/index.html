Privacy and Card Retention

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to The Marker web, iPhone, Android and Apple Watch features

The Marker is a golf scorekeeping app that works with The Scorecard or independently. It does not use advertising or advertising tracking. This policy explains which card information stays on your device, which information is sent to the shared service, and what the app's different Delete actions actually remove.

Most important: The new 30-day cleanup applies to temporary Caddie and Follower live copies stored on the server. It does not automatically delete the last 30 finished cards saved on your phone, posted rounds in The Scorecard, or posting records.

1. Information used

Depending on how you use The Marker, the app handles the course, tee, date, golfer names, handicaps, hole scores and totals. If you sign in with The Scorecard, it also uses your email account and Firebase account identifier, group memberships and relevant golfer identifiers. Guest play does not require your Scorecard email-and-password account; limited anonymous sign-in may still be used for online features.

The Marker uses this information to save a round, synchronize caddie/follower scores when you choose that feature, and post eligible member rounds to The Scorecard. A Caddie/Follower live copy contains the course, tee, players' names, course handicaps and scores needed for the shared view; it also records the account identifier of the person who shared it, but not the playing group's internal golfer identifiers.

2. Cards saved on your phone or browser

30 finished cardsThe Marker keeps up to the most recent 30 completed/retained cards on that device. Saving another can displace the oldest.
No automatic age limitThese local cards are not removed simply because 30 days pass. The current round, offline queue and app preferences are also saved locally as needed.

Local storage belongs to that particular browser, phone or app installation. A card saved on one device is not automatically a backup on another. Clearing site/app storage or uninstalling the app can remove local copies. You can also delete eligible local cards yourself.

Typed guest names and their scores are kept on the card locally and may appear when you deliberately share a text summary or a live view. Only member golfers' rounds are included in a normal posting to The Scorecard.

3. Temporary Caddie and Follower copies

Only when a player chooses Caddie or Follower sharing, The Marker creates a temporary live copy on its server (a markerLive record). A six-character code or QR link points to that copy. It contains enough of the card for a caddie to enter scores or a follower to watch them; the follower cannot edit the card.

  1. Access ends after 12 hours at the latest. A code normally expires 12 hours after it was created. Caddie access can end earlier if the golfer takes back the card or the round is finished. The Watch app also stops following after a round is posted.
  2. Expiry is not immediate deletion. The temporary live copy and its own code record can remain stored after the link stops working.
  3. New 30-day cleanup. After the live copy's access-expiry time is at least 30 days old, the server's daily cleanup makes it eligible for deletion together with its Caddie and Follower code records.
  4. Deletion may happen later. The scheduled cleanup processes up to 200 eligible cards in one run, and a changed record or failed run is left for another run. Thirty days is therefore the cleanup threshold, not a guaranteed exact erasure deadline.

Who can read a Follower card? Anyone with its valid follow code can view the shared live scores, even if that person is not listed as a golfer on the card. Share the code or QR link only with people you intend to allow. Online features require a connection.

4. Posted cards and Scorecard records

When you post an eligible member round, The Marker submits the member golfers' card details to its server, and The Scorecard stores the resulting rounds and handicap history. The Marker also keeps a submission record to verify posting, corrections, deleted rounds and re-posting.

The 30-day Caddie/Follower cleanup never deletes posted rounds or submission records. Posted golf history is governed by The Scorecard's access and deletion rules. The Marker code does not set a general automatic expiration date for those posted records. See The Scorecard Privacy Policy for how a Scorecard account and shared history are handled.

5. What the deletion buttons really do

Delete this cardFor an unposted card that is not actively being sent, removes that card from this device's saved list. It does not promise erasure of any earlier server submission attempt.
Remove from this phoneRemoves a posted card's local copy only. Its rounds and submission record remain in The Scorecard.
Delete its rounds from The ScorecardFor authorized users, deletes the rounds belonging to that posted card and recalculates affected handicap information. The submission record remains so the missing rounds can be identified for possible re-posting.
Delete this card for goodWhen the card's Scorecard rounds were already deleted, removes it from the visible re-post lists and this device, and prevents re-posting. A server submission record still keeps a discard audit entry; this is not a purge of all server data.

Hide on a card with deleted rounds is different again: it hides the item from a list and does not delete a round or submission. The scorer generally has the first 24 hours after posting to make authorized corrections/deletions; group owners or administrators may have later authority, subject to the app's checks.

Before deleting: Check whether you are removing a device copy, deleting posted rounds, or merely removing a re-post option. These actions are not interchangeable.

6. Text sharing, Apple Watch and card photos

  • Messages and shared text: When you choose WhatsApp, email, text or another share method, the copied scorecard is handled by that service and recipient. Removing the card in Marker does not recall their copies.
  • Apple Watch: The paired Watch app receives the active follower card and can keep a local copy for its display and widgets. It stops following after the posted/expired state; complication updates may lag behind the open app.
  • Photographs of printed club cards: If you choose to scan a course card, the image is sent to the Cloudflare AI service to read course figures. The Marker code stores extracted figures for verification but does not save the original photograph as a retained Marker card image.

7. Services, security, and questions

The Marker relies on Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for sign-in and golf data, and a Cloudflare Worker for card sharing, posting, cleanup and optional scorecard-photo reading. Course search may use a course-information provider. Providers may process routine connection information under their own policies. The app does not sell card data or use advertising tracking.

The privacy manifest for the iPhone app declares account email, name, user identifier, app content and optional images used for app functionality, not tracking.

Children. The Marker is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
