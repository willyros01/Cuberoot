Fairpot User Guide

Split shared expenses fairly — in any currency. For the Fairpot app on iPhone and iPad, version 0.5.1.

In five steps

  1. Describe the expense: description, date, how many people are splitting it (including you) and the currency you paid in.
  2. Add the receipts: a category and amount for each part of the bill, with a photo if you like.
  3. Check the shares: what each person pays, in the expense currency and in your home currency.
  4. Save and share: tap Save, then Share to send everyone what they owe and how to pay you.
  5. Look back: History lists every expense; Summary shows totals by category, month and year.

First launch

Fairpot first shows its Terms of Use. Tick I have read and agree, then tap Accept. Fairpot stays locked until the terms are accepted.

Your data stays on your device. Fairpot has no account and no cloud. Use Summary → Save backup → Save to Files now and then to keep a copy.
