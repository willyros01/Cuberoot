FIDUNIO Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

FIDUNIO is a private messaging service. It does not use advertising or advertising tracking.

Information FIDUNIO stores

  • Account information such as email address, display name, optional telephone number, Firebase user ID, account role and access status.
  • Device and notification registration information needed to deliver notifications and protect account access.
  • End-to-end encryption public-key and recovery metadata. FIDUNIO does not store your six-digit PIN in readable form.
  • Encrypted messages and encrypted attachments needed to provide messaging, group history and delivery features.
  • Group membership, invitations, account-deletion state, blocked-user records and abuse reports that you choose to submit.

End-to-end encrypted conversations

FIDUNIO encrypts supported message content before it is stored in Firebase. System administrators do not receive a general right to read conversations merely because they are administrators. Access to conversation records is restricted by account and conversation or group membership rules.

Services used

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: authentication, account records, encrypted message and attachment storage, Cloud Functions, notifications, App Check and related service operation.
  • Apple: TestFlight/App Store distribution, push-notification delivery and App Attest or DeviceCheck on supported devices.

These providers may process routine service and connection information under their own terms.

Abuse reports and blocking

When you use Report Abuse, FIDUNIO stores the report category, optional reported user, optional notes, your account ID and the moderation status. Unrelated conversation content is not automatically copied into the report. Blocked-user records are private to the blocking account and are used to prevent new direct contact.

Account deletion

Use Settings → Delete My Account to initiate permanent account deletion. FIDUNIO requires current-account verification and performs controlled cleanup before deleting the Firebase Authentication account. A user who owns a group must first delete or otherwise resolve that owned group so other members' shared history is not silently destroyed.

Deletion removes the account's personal profile, notification registrations, account security/recovery records and the user's own sent messages through the controlled deletion process. Shared records that must remain for other users may be retained only in a form that no longer represents an active FIDUNIO account.

Retention

FIDUNIO retains information while needed to operate the account and messaging service, to satisfy user-selected history settings, to process deletion, or where limited retention is needed for security and abuse-handling purposes.

Children

FIDUNIO is not directed to children and is intended for invited users who are permitted to use the service.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will show a new effective date and FIDUNIO may require renewed acknowledgement in the app. Questions may be sent to willyros01@gmail.com.
