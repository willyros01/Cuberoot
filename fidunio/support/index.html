FIDUNIO Support

For help, email willyros01@gmail.com. Include what you were doing, the device type, and the exact message shown. Do not send your password, six-digit FIDUNIO PIN, recovery secrets or invitation code.

Report abuse

Open Settings → Safety → Report Abuse. Choose a reason, optionally identify the person involved, and add brief notes. Administrators receive the report without receiving unrelated conversation contents.

Block a user

Open Settings → Safety → Blocked Users. Blocking prevents new direct contact in both directions. Shared group membership is managed separately by group administrators.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete My Account, verify your account with your current password and FIDUNIO PIN, and follow the deletion steps. If you own a group, resolve that ownership first.

Sign-in and PIN

Your account password authenticates you to Firebase. Your six-digit FIDUNIO PIN protects local secure messaging and recovery. Do not share either one.

Notifications

Enable notifications in FIDUNIO Settings and in iOS Settings. A notification tap should open the intended FIDUNIO conversation after local unlock when required.
