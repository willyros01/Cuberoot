The Scorecard

Android Quick Start — Amazon Fire HD 8 and Android tablets

Based on Scorecard v2.30.1-android.1 · Android test edition · October 2026

The Scorecard tracks golf rounds, handicaps, games, and rankings for groups of golfers.

1. Install The Scorecard

  1. On your Fire or Android tablet, visit www.cuberoot-systems.com and find The Scorecard under Apps.
  2. When the Android test APK is available, choose Download Android APK.
  3. Open the downloaded file. If Fire OS asks permission to install from your browser, allow it and continue.
  4. Tap Open when installation finishes.
Important: Future updates should install over the existing app. Do not uninstall first; keep any exported backup files safe. This is a test build, not a Google Play Store release.

2. Sign in or request access

  1. Sign in with your existing Scorecard email and password.
  2. If you are new, use the application or invitation process shown on the sign-in screen.
  3. For an invited private group, use the one-time invitation link. Create your account or sign in, then complete joining.
  4. A consumed invitation cannot be used again. Do not send personal invitation links to others.
Privacy: Regular golfers have access to their own detailed rounds and relevant group rankings. Admins and owners have additional group-management features.

3. Record a golf round

  1. Open Enter and select the golfer, course, tee, and date as requested.
  2. Enter the gross score and any other required details.
  3. Check the information and tap the button to post the round.
  4. Open History to verify that the round appears correctly.

The app has an offline queue. If you are disconnected, let it reconnect and confirm the operation has been sent; do not repeatedly post the same round.

4. View handicaps, games, and rankings

  1. Use the available tabs to view your handicap information and recent rounds.
  2. Open a group game to see its results or leaderboard.
  3. Open group rankings to compare members' indexes as permitted by your role.

5. Make the text larger

  1. Tap the A text-size button in the app header.
  2. Choose A+ or A++ for easier reading.
  3. The Appearance button cycles through automatic, light, and dark styles.

6. Save a backup

  1. In Scorecard, open its backup/export option where available to your role.
  2. Tap Save backup to Downloads. This should not open the Share menu.
  3. The app should confirm the exact saved filename. Open your Fire tablet's Downloads folder and find the matching scorecard-*.txt file.
  4. Confirm the file is not empty, then copy it somewhere safe outside the tablet. If Scorecard displays Backup was NOT saved, do not assume a file exists.
A file export does not replace the normal Firebase synchronization. Before deleting or reinstalling the app, confirm the backup is accessible and that online data is current.

7. Invitations and external links

Invitation links use Cuberoot's Scorecard join address. With Android App Links correctly verified, an invitation can open the installed Scorecard app. Otherwise, it may open in the browser. Follow the instructions displayed and keep one-use invitations private.

Support, privacy, and guide links open in a Fire OS browser rather than inside Scorecard.

8. Troubleshooting

Sign-in fails: Check Internet access, email/password, and any pending account approval. Do not create duplicate accounts to work around a sign-in problem.

Round not visible: Check connection and queue status, then refresh History before attempting to post again.

External website does not open: Ensure Silk or another browser is installed, then retry. Do not uninstall the Scorecard app.

Backup missing: Check Downloads in the Fire file manager. If the app reported an error, the backup was not confirmed.

Android acceptance testing: the release must be checked on a real Fire HD 8 before full Android parity is claimed.
