SpendRecorder User Guide

Version 1.1.0 · iPhone, iPad and Android

Record personal expenses in different currencies, attach receipt photos and see totals in your home currency. Your records stay on your device.

Quick start

  1. Open SpendRecorder and complete its first-use Terms screen if shown.
  2. On New, choose the date, currency and amount.
  3. Select a vendor and category. Use + Add new vendor… or + Add new category… when needed.
  4. Add comments or a receipt photo if useful.
  5. For a repeating bill, tick Recurring expense and select its frequency.
  6. Tap Add expense. Find the saved record in History and totals in Summary.

Automatic posting

The expense date is the starting date. When you open or resume the app, due expenses are added to History and totals automatically. Missed dates are caught up too. There is no review or confirmation step.

If the app is fully closed, posting happens when you next open it. Each occurrence is its own unique record; repeatedly opening the app does not add duplicates.

A monthly date on the 29th–31st uses the last available day in a shorter month, then returns to the original day when possible. Leap-day yearly expenses follow the same principle.

Editing a recurring expense

In History, tap Edit on the occurrence you want to change.

The latest occurrence controls future postings. Editing its amount, date or frequency updates that occurrence and the future template. Earlier recorded expenses stay unchanged.

An amount-only edit keeps the existing calendar cadence. A date or frequency change establishes the future cadence from the latest occurrence’s date without reposting dates already processed.

Older occurrences can still be corrected individually. Their recurring controls are disabled because a correction to a past record must not change future postings or create another schedule.

Stop repeating

Edit the latest occurrence, untick Recurring expense, then tap Save changes. Recorded expenses remain in History.

Delete one occurrence

Deleting an automatically posted occurrence leaves other records and future postings unchanged. Deleting the original expense that owns the schedule stops future postings. Use the existing Undo prompt to reverse an accidental deletion before it is committed.

Receipt photos

On iPhone/iPad, tap Add photo and choose a photo using the device’s picker. On Android, tap Add photo (Camera / Gallery) to take a photo or choose an existing one. Preview the photo before saving.

Photos stay attached to their own expense. Automatic future occurrences do not reuse a previous receipt; add the appropriate new receipt afterward if needed.

History and Summary

History lists saved expenses. Search vendor names, categories or notes, filter by category, and use Edit, Share or Delete on an entry. Selection controls let you work with several entries.

Summary shows totals and breakdowns by period, category and vendor. Your home currency determines the displayed totals. Changing it does not rewrite the original amount or saved exchange rate of an older expense.

Exchange rates

Each expense keeps its original currency and saved rate. A non-home-currency expense shows the rate source. You can enter your own rate when needed. Offline automatic entries use the existing cached-rate fallback, labelled in the record; a placeholder is shown when no usable rate is available.

Export and import

In Summary, export your expense records as Excel or CSV. iPhone/iPad use the available sharing and Save to Files choices. Android saves the export to Downloads and confirms the result.

Version 1.1 exports include a Recurring Data column containing schedule identity, progress and future defaults. Keep that column intact if you intend to restore recurring schedules. Export all records to include all original schedule owners.

Use Import file in Summary to import a supported CSV or Excel export. Exact duplicate entries are skipped. Older exports without recurring metadata import as ordinary expenses.

CSV/Excel exports contain expense data and scheduling metadata; they do not contain receipt photo files. Keep regular device backups as well.

Installing updates

iPhone/iPad: update through TestFlight when the new build is available.

Android: download the signed APK from the SpendRecorder card on Cuberoot and install it over the existing app. Do not uninstall first—uninstalling can remove local records and receipts.

Open Cuberoot app downloads

SpendRecorder requires no user account or Firebase sign-in. Expenses and receipts are stored locally in SQLite on the phone or tablet.
