Quick Start

How to use The Scorecard on iPhone, iPad or the web, step by step.

About the pictures: every picture is a real screen of The Scorecard 2.31.4, in its Lake blue look, with made-up golfers. Updated 13 November 2026.

1. Join The Scorecard

Regular user

Sign in, or ask to join.

  1. Have an account? Type your email and Scorecard password and tap Sign in.
  2. Invited by your group? Tap the link you were sent: it names you, so there is nothing to type.
  3. Given a group code? Tap I have a code. Otherwise tap Become a member or request a group.
  4. Forgot your password? Type your email and tap Forgot the password, then follow the email.

2. Post your first round

Regular user

Three short steps on the Enter tab.

  1. Tap Enter. Choose the course and the tees you played, then tap Next.
  2. Type your score. If the round was part of a game, choose the game.
  3. Tap Post round once.

3. Check your handicap and history

Regular user

My rounds and Summary.

  1. My rounds lists every round, with a chart of your handicap index. Tap a dot to see that round.
  2. A round scored in The Marker shows ▦ Card: tap it for every hole.
  3. Summary shows the group ranking by handicap index.

4. Admin: invite a golfer

Administrator

Invitations are sent from the Manage tab.

  1. Tap Manage, then Show beside Golfers in this group.
  2. Tap Invite beside the golfer, choose Guest or Admin, and tap Choose how to send it.
  3. A tick ✓ beside a name means that golfer has joined.

5. Admin: correct a score

Administrator

Corrections are made from History.

  1. Tap History and find the round. Tap Edit.
  2. Change what is wrong and save. Rounds scored in The Marker are corrected in The Marker.

6. Delete your own account

Regular user

At the bottom of every page.

  1. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  2. Type your Scorecard password and confirm. Your golfer name, rounds and handicap stay with the group.
  3. Tap Keep it to stop. The full guide explains what is removed and what stays.
