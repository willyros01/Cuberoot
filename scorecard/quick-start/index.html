The Scorecard
Quick Start Guide

Simple illustrated instructions for golfers and group administrators on iPhone and iPad.

Regular user — start here

📱
1. Open Scorecard

Open The Scorecard app and sign in with your account.

✉️
2. Join a group

Open the invitation from your group administrator and follow the Join instructions.

🏌️
3. Find your golfer

Choose your group. Check your name, handicap and recent rounds.

Record a golf round

📝
Enter

Open the entry screen and choose the golfer.

⛳
Course and tee

Select the course and tee, then enter the adjusted score.

✅
Post and check

Tap Post round. Open History and confirm the round appears.

If you are offline, keep the app installed. Reconnect and check that the round has synced before clearing any device data.

Administrator — start here

👥
1. Open your group

Select the golf group you administer.

⚙️
2. Open Manage

Review the roster and group settings available to your role.

🔗
3. Invite golfers

Use the invitation action for the intended golfer and send the link privately.

Admin: correct a score

📚
Find the round

Open the group's History and find the entry.

✏️
Edit

Open the available edit action, make the correction and save.

🔎
Verify

Confirm the new score and handicap are displayed correctly.
