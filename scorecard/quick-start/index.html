The Scorecard
Illustrated Quick Start

How to use The Scorecard on iPhone or iPad, with illustrated screens and numbered tap instructions.

About the illustrations: The screens below are reconstructed from The Scorecard’s current interface and use fictional names and scores. Orange numbered highlights show exactly which control to use. They are teaching illustrations, not captures of a personal account.

1. Join The Scorecard

Regular user
  1. 1 — If you already have an account, enter your email and password and select Sign in.
  2. 2 — If you are new, select Become a member or start a group, then choose Become a member. Enter your full name and email, accept the conditions and submit. Follow the email you receive to finish setting your password.
  3. 3 — If your private group gave you a code, tap I have a code. If you have an invitation link, open that link instead.

2. Post your first round

Regular user
  1. Open the Enter tab. For a regular golfer, the one-step-at-a-time entry starts with your golfer already chosen.
  2. 1 — Select the golf course.
  3. 2 — Tap the tee you played.
  4. 3 — Tap Next, type your score on step 3, then tap Post round.

3. Check your handicap and history

Regular user
  1. Open Summary to see My season and your handicap index. Three recorded rounds are needed before an initial index can appear.
  2. 1 — Your own handicap index is displayed prominently.
  3. 2 — Review the group ranking. In a public group, other golfers' names and indexes are shown; their individual rounds are private.
  4. Open My rounds to see your scores and use the year and course filters.

4. Admin: invite a golfer

Administrator
  1. Open your group's Manage tab. Review Golfers in this group.
  2. 1–2 — Find the correct golfer and tap Invite. Send the generated personal invitation privately.
  3. 3 — If the invitation has already been sent, use Re-send. Check that the golfer eventually shows as Joined.
  4. Open Admin → Members to review joined members and remove a regular member when necessary.

5. Admin: correct a score

Administrator
  1. Open History, not My rounds; admins can see group rounds.
  2. 1 — Use the golfer filter to locate the correct person.
  3. 2 — Select Edit on the appropriate round.
  4. 3 — Correct the details and tap Save changes. Reopen History and check the result.

6. Delete your own account

Regular user
  1. Open The Scorecard, go to the bottom of any signed-in screen and tap 1 — Delete my account. This is available to a regular user or an administrator; you do not need an admin to start it.
  2. Read the confirmation. If asked, type your Scorecard password and tap Delete my account again. Tap Keep it if you decide not to proceed.
  3. Your sign-in and group memberships are removed. Your golfer name, rounds and handicap history remain in shared golf records. An administrator may continue entering rounds for that golfer.
  4. If the app says you own a group, the request is refused. Handle that group first, with a verified backup, or contact Support. If rounds are waiting to sync, connect and wait for Synced.
  5. For a stopped or incomplete deletion, see the full recovery instructions.

Not the same thing: Remove from group is an administrator action that removes one member’s access to one group. Delete my account is self-service and removes your sign-in from every group.
