The Scorecard — Quick Start

Golf scores, handicaps and shared group history on iPhone, iPad and the web.

App Store launch is being prepared. Until the public iOS app is released, existing testers can use TestFlight and existing members can continue using the web app.

1. Open The Scorecard

  1. Existing TestFlight testers: open the installed Scorecard app.
  2. Web users: open The Scorecard web app.
  3. Already have an account? Sign in with your existing Scorecard email and password. Do not create a duplicate golfer.

2. Join your golf group

  1. If your group owner invited you, open the invitation link sent to you.
  2. Follow the join instructions and confirm your golfer name.
  3. Choose your group and check that the roster and history are correct.

Public-download onboarding is being finalized for App Store launch. Being able to download the app does not automatically grant access to a private group.

3. View your handicap

Open your group and find your golfer name. You can view the handicap index calculated from the recorded rounds. If the history seems incomplete, contact your group admin before creating a new golfer.

4. Enter a round

  1. Open the entry screen.
  2. Select your golfer, course and tee.
  3. Enter the adjusted score and post the round.
  4. Check History to confirm the round appears.

5. Using Scorecard offline

If your device has no connection, a round may be saved on the device for later upload. Reconnect, reopen the app and confirm it has synced before deleting the app or clearing its local data.

6. Moving from the website to iPhone or iPad

Sign in with the same account on both platforms. Before switching, confirm the web app shows Synced and save a backup. Keep the web app until your group, rounds and handicap match on the new device. See the full migration instructions.

7. Help, privacy and account deletion

For help, visit Scorecard Support or email willyros01@gmail.com. Never send a password or invitation code. For account deletion, find Delete my account at the bottom of the app and read the confirmation carefully. Shared golf history may remain after account deletion as described in the Privacy Policy.
