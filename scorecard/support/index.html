I cannot enter my score

On the Enter tab, check the golfer, course, tee and score. A regular golfer must be correctly linked to their account. If the app says you are not linked to a golfer, ask the group admin for a personal invitation.

My round did not appear

Open My rounds or History, clear the year and course filters and check the sync status. A round entered offline may not be uploaded until the connection returns. Do not post the round a second time without checking for it first.

My handicap is missing or wrong

Confirm the recorded rounds and tees. The app needs at least three rounds for an initial handicap index. If a score is wrong, an administrator may correct it in History → Edit.

I cannot see Admin

The Admin and Manage tabs appear only for appropriate group roles. Ask the group owner to review your membership.

Account deletion

  1. Open The Scorecard.
  2. Find Delete my account at the bottom of an app screen.
  3. Read the explanation, confirm and follow the app instructions.

If you own a group, the app requires you to resolve your owned groups first. Shared golfer names, rounds and handicap history may remain; see the Privacy Policy.
