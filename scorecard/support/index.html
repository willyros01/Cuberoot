I cannot enter my score

On the Enter tab, check the golfer, course, tee and score. A regular golfer must be correctly linked to their account. If the app says you are not linked to a golfer, ask the group admin for a personal invitation.

My round did not appear

Open My rounds or History, clear the year and course filters and check the sync status. A round entered offline may not be uploaded until the connection returns. Do not post the round a second time without checking for it first.

My handicap is missing or wrong

Confirm the recorded rounds and tees. The app needs at least three rounds for an initial handicap index. If a score is wrong, an administrator may correct it in History → Edit.

I cannot see Admin

The Admin and Manage tabs appear only for appropriate group roles. Ask your group administrator to review your membership.

Self-service account deletion

See the illustrated Quick Start deletion walkthrough or the complete illustrated deletion instructions.

  1. While signed in, scroll to the bottom of any main screen. Tap Delete my account.
  2. Read the warning. Enter your Scorecard password when asked, then tap Delete my account again. Keep it cancels.
  3. If deletion succeeds, the app shows Your account has been deleted.

The account and its memberships are removed, but shared golfer names, rounds and handicap history remain. If the app reports a restriction related to a group you manage, contact Support. Pending round uploads must sync before deleting.

If you see Your account isn't deleted yet, reconnect and tap Try again; the app provides an automated completion path if repeated attempts fail. If you see This account is being deleted, you have been signed out while completion continues automatically.

Remove from group in the Admin area is a different action: it revokes membership of one group without deleting the person's account.
