For help, email willyros01@gmail.com. Include what you were doing and the exact message shown. Do not send your password or an invitation code.

Delete your account

  1. Open The Scorecard.
  2. At the bottom of any screen, tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what will remain, then confirm.

If you own a group, first delete that group; ownership cannot be transferred. Deletion normally finishes immediately; if interrupted, the automated completion process finishes within one day.

Move from the web app to the iPhone app

Your existing account and golf data stay in Firebase; you do not export or re-enter them.

  1. Install The Scorecard from TestFlight or the App Store.
  2. If you already use an email and password, sign in with the same email and password.
  3. If you are a guest without a password, keep using the web app until your group owner sends the moving invitation described in the guide. Open that invitation on the iPhone and complete the move once.
  4. Confirm the correct group, golfer and recent rounds appear before removing the old home-screen icon.
