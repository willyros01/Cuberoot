Full Illustrated User Guide

A visual, step-by-step manual for regular members, administrators and group owners on iPhone and iPad.

About the illustrations: The screens below are reconstructed from The Scorecard’s current interface and use fictional names and scores. Orange numbered highlights show exactly which control to use. They are teaching illustrations, not captures of a personal account.

Who can do what?

Regular user View your rounds, post your own rounds, follow your handicap and join groups to which you have access.

Administrator Manage the group roster, invite golfers, create games, post or correct group scores and review members. Some controls depend on the group's permissions.

Owner An owner additionally sees owner-only Settings and special group-management tools. The service-wide owner can also manage public-group applications and group requests.

1. Open the app and sign in

Regular user
  1. Existing member: enter your email and Scorecard password, then tap Sign in.
  2. First-time applicant: tap Become a member or start a group. If you have a private invitation, use it instead.
  3. If you were given a group code, tap I have a code and follow the instructions.

2. Apply to become a public-group member

Regular user
  1. Tap Become a member or start a group → Become a member.
  2. 1–2 — Enter your full name and email address. No password is requested at this step.
  3. 3–4 — Read the conditions, check your agreement and tap Send my application.
  4. Watch for the email that lets you confirm your address and create a Scorecard password. Depending on the application settings, entry may be automatic or reviewed by an administrator.

3. Join an invited private group

Regular user
  1. Open the personal invitation link sent by your organiser.
  2. 1–4 — If you are new, create an account with your email and a new Scorecard password; then follow the Join the group prompt.
  3. 5 — If you already have an account, use Have an account? Sign in.
  4. Confirm your golfer name and group before posting a round. Do not create a duplicate golfer.

4. Enter a round — guided steps

Regular user
  1. Tap the bottom Enter tab. In guided entry, regular members have their golfer fixed automatically.
  2. The date appears near the top; tap it if you need another round date.
  3. 1 — Choose your course; 2 — choose the tees; 3 — tap Next.
  4. Type the score on step 3, choose the game if relevant and tap Post round. Only post once, even if temporarily offline.

5. Finish and post the score

Regular user
  1. 1 — Type the gross score shown on the actual card.
  2. 2 — Select a game only if this round belongs in that event. Otherwise leave Not part of a game.
  3. 3 — Tap Post round. The app calculates the round differential and updates the handicap as appropriate.
  4. Wait for Synced before removing the app or clearing local data. Check the round in My rounds.

6. Find a round in My rounds

Regular user
  1. Tap My rounds at the bottom of the app.
  2. 1–2 — Choose a year or course to narrow the results.
  3. 3 — Review the date, course, tee and score. If an editable action is available for your own round, use it within the permitted correction window.
  4. If the wrong golfer or score is shown, contact the group administrator.

7. Read your handicap and group ranking

Regular user
  1. Tap Summary. The My season section shows your rounds and current handicap index.
  2. 1 — Find your large Your handicap index value. With too few qualifying rounds, an index is not shown yet.
  3. 2 — Read the group ranking. For public-group members, other golfers' individual round histories are not exposed.
  4. 3 — Use the By year drill-down to browse your season.

8. Open games and scoreboards

Regular user
  1. Tap Games in the bottom navigation.
  2. 1–2 — Tap a named game to open its participants and published results.
  3. If you are entering a score for an organised game, select the matching game when posting the round.
  4. If no games are listed, ask your organiser to create one.

9. Admin: enter a round for any golfer

Administrator
  1. Open Enter. Admins normally see the full entry form.
  2. 1–2 — Choose the correct date and golfer.
  3. 3–4 — Choose the course and tee. Check the rating and slope.
  4. 5 — Enter gross score and adjusted score if different.
  5. 6 — Tap Post round and verify the new History entry.

10. Admin: correct or remove a round

Administrator
  1. Open History and filter by the correct golfer.
  2. 2 — Tap Edit, correct the field and tap Save changes.
  3. Use Delete only when the round truly must be removed; check confirmation carefully.
  4. Reopen History and Summary to verify the affected score and handicap.

11. Admin: rankings and sharing

Administrator
  1. Open Summary → Rankings.
  2. 1 — Select a year and either the whole year or a specific month.
  3. The admin ranking shows gross and net statistics; a golfer may need at least three rounds to be ranked.
  4. 2 — Tap Share to send the results.

12. Admin: create a game

Administrator
  1. Open the Games tab.
  2. 1 — Tap Add a game.
  3. 2–4 — Choose the date, course and optional name. There is also an option for a multi-day event.
  4. 5 — Tap Save game. Members can now associate their entered rounds with this game.

13. Admin: invite and track golfers

Administrator
  1. Open Manage. The Golfers in this group roster appears.
  2. Check whether the golfer is already Joined, Invited, or not yet invited.
  3. 2 — Tap Invite for the correct golfer; send their link privately. Use Re-send rather than creating an unnecessary duplicate.
  4. You can add a golfer only when needed. Always search the existing roster first.

14. Admin: manage courses and tees

Administrator
  1. In Manage, find the Courses section.
  2. 1 — Tap Add a course; 2 — search by name or enter the course details manually.
  3. 3 — Confirm the selected tees' rating, slope and par before using the course.
  4. 4 — Use Edit only where permitted. Existing rounds retain the course information recorded on the day they were played.

15. Admin: read the Cockpit

Administrator
  1. Tap the bottom Admin tab, then Cockpit.
  2. 1 — Review member counts, recent activity, invitations not used and rounds posted this month.
  3. 2 — Review Last seen to understand member activity.
  4. For service-wide owners, the Cockpit may summarise multiple groups rather than just the current one.

16. Admin: manage group members

Administrator
  1. Tap Admin → Members.
  2. 1 — Find the member's name and role. Confirm the exact person before changing access.
  3. 2 — Use Remove from group when a regular member should lose access. Shared rounds are retained.
  4. Only owner-level functions can change owner-level settings; the admin screen intentionally does not display unavailable controls.

17. Public-group Admin: applications and reports

Administrator
  1. For the public group, open Admin → Applications. This tab is not necessarily shown for private-group administrators.
  2. 1 — Review the pending applications; approve or decline as allowed by your role.
  3. 2 — Review reports and block-list information when available.
  4. The service owner can control manual or automatic application processing.

18. Owner-only settings and backup

Owner
  1. If you are the group owner, open Admin → Settings. This tab is not shown to ordinary group administrators.
  2. 1–4 — Open Group, Backup, Course lookup or Account as needed.
  3. Save your backup and verify it is readable before any destructive administrative action.
  4. Group deletion is especially consequential; review the warning and do not treat it like removing a member.

19. Self-service deletion: where to find it

Regular user
  1. Open The Scorecard while signed in. The Delete my account link is in the small footer below the content of every main screen. Scroll right to the bottom.
  2. Tap 1 — Delete my account. You do not need an administrator’s permission to request deletion of your own account.
  3. A confirmation sheet opens. This is not the same as signing out, deleting the app or asking an administrator to remove you from a single group.

20. Read the warning and confirm deletion

Regular user
  1. The screen says Delete your account?. Read the consequences: your email and sign-in are deleted, and you leave every group. The action cannot be undone.
  2. 2 — Enter your Scorecard password if the password field appears. This is not your email account password.
  3. 3 — Tap Delete my account to proceed. The app checks ownership, flushes pending changes and verifies your identity before removing access.
  4. 4 — Choose Keep it to cancel before proceeding. A successfully finished deletion shows Your account has been deleted, then Done.

21. What is removed — and what stays

Removed: your Scorecard sign-in, account email, group memberships, accepted invitation claims and the link between your account and golfer record.

Retained for other group members: your golfer name, entered scores, round history and handicap information. These remain shared golf records, and an administrator may keep entering that golfer's scores.

Deleting your Scorecard account does not erase the shared golf history. See the Scorecard Privacy Policy.

Before deleting

  1. Ensure your app is online and shows Synced. Changes waiting to upload block deletion.
  2. If the app warns that earlier changes could not be saved, review them carefully. Dismiss them discards those unsaved changes; do not press it unless you accept losing them.
  3. If you are a group owner, the app refuses deletion until your owned group is deleted. Back up and verify the group data before considering such a destructive step. Contact Support if unsure.

Account deletion versus group removal

Self-service account deletion: removes your personal sign-in and leaves all groups.

Admin → Members → Remove from group: removes another regular member’s access to the current group while retaining their golfer and rounds. It does not delete their Scorecard account.

Remove from my list: only hides a group from your switcher; it does not remove your membership or delete your account.

22. If deletion stops or displays an error

Regular user
  1. If the app says Your account isn't deleted yet, you are still signed in. Tap 1 — Try again once you are online. If it repeatedly fails, the automated completion process is intended to finish within a day.
  2. If the app says This account is being deleted, you have already been signed out. The app says it will finish automatically, normally within 20 minutes. Do not create a replacement account immediately.
  3. A wrong password returns an error and should leave the account unchanged. Enter the correct Scorecard password instead.
  4. If the app shows Send a report for a failed deletion check, use that option. For a lingering problem, contact Scorecard Support and include the exact error without sharing your password.

23. Troubleshooting and account help

My round is missing

Check My rounds or History, clear date/course filters and confirm the device is synced. Do not post a duplicate just because the connection was lost.

I cannot post a score

Check the golfer, course, tee and score. A regular user whose account is not linked to their golfer must ask the group organiser to provide the correct personal invitation.

I cannot see Admin

Only users with an appropriate management role receive that tab. Ask the group owner to review your account's role.

My handicap is blank

An initial index normally needs at least three recorded rounds. Verify the golfer and rounds first.

Delete my account

See the illustrated self-service deletion instructions, including confirmation, retained records, ownership restrictions and recovery.
