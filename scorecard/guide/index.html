The Scorecard User Guide

Quick start for the iPhone app and the web app.

Download the PDF guide

Start or join

A group owner creates the group and sends invitation links. A golfer normally joins by opening their named invitation. Use the same invitation only for the person named in it.

Use the iPhone app

  1. Install The Scorecard from TestFlight or the App Store.
  2. Open an invitation link. If the app is installed, the iPhone opens it directly; otherwise the web app opens.
  3. When asked, sign in with the same email and password you use on the web.
  4. Choose your group and confirm your golfer name and recent rounds.

Use the web app

Open willyros01.github.io/scorecard. You may continue using the web app after the iPhone app is available. Both use the same Firebase account and shared group data.

Moving from the web app to the iPhone app

Do not create a second golfer. The move connects the iPhone app to the account and golfer you already use.

  • You already have an email and password: install the iPhone app and sign in with exactly the same email and password.
  • You are a guest without a password: continue on the web until the group owner uses the moving/re-invitation option and sends you the new invitation. Open it on the iPhone, follow the prompts and confirm your existing golfer and rounds appear.
  • You use more than one device: use the same account on each device. Do not accept another person's invitation.

Before removing the web home-screen icon, confirm the same group, golfer, handicap and recent rounds appear on the iPhone.

Enter and correct a round

  1. Open the group and choose the entry screen.
  2. Select the golfer, course and tee; enter the adjusted score.
  3. Post the round. If offline, leave the app installed and reconnect later so the queued round can upload.
  4. A golfer can correct their own round during the 24-hour window. Group administrators can make later corrections.

Handicap and group history

The Scorecard calculates handicap from stored rounds using the World Handicap System method used by the app. A golfer may play in several groups while keeping one golfer record and one handicap history.

Account deletion

Open Settings and choose Delete my account. Owners must first transfer or delete owned groups. Your account connection is removed, but your golfer name, rounds and handicap history stay in the shared golf record.
