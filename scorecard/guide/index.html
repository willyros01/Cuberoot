The Scorecard
Full User Guide

Using the production iPhone and iPad app — illustrated instructions for Regular Users, Administrators and Group Owners.

1. Understanding the roles

🏌️ Regular User: view the group, your scores, handicaps and history, and enter rounds where permitted.

🛠️ Administrator: manage golfers and invitations, review entries and correct scores with the group's admin permissions.

👑 Owner: manages the group itself and decides who has administrator access.

2. Regular User: sign in and join

📱
Open app

Launch The Scorecard and choose the sign-in option matching your account.

✉️
Use invitation

Open the link your group sent you and complete the join process.

👤
Check identity

Confirm your group name and golfer profile before entering scores.

A group invitation is personal. Do not forward another golfer's invitation or create a second golfer if your record is already present.

3. Regular User: view handicap and scores

👥
Group and roster

Choose your group and locate yourself in the golfer list.

📊
Handicap

Read the handicap index next to your golfer details. It is based on recorded score differentials.

📚
History

Open History to review dates, courses, scores and prior rounds.

4. Regular User: enter a round

📝
Enter

Open the entry screen, choose the golfer and select the round date.

⛳
Course and tee

Select the correct course and tee, including the rating/slope associated with that tee.

✅
Save and verify

Enter the adjusted score, post the round, then check History and handicap.

If the app reports a pending or saved-on-device round, reconnect to the network and confirm it finishes syncing. Do not submit the same round again merely because it was temporarily offline.

5. Regular User: review games and standings

Open the relevant group game, tournament or rankings view if provided by your group. Check the event, participants and net or gross results before sharing. Contact an administrator if a round appears in the wrong event.

6. Administrator: manage the roster

👥
Select group

Switch to the group you administer.

⚙️
Manage → People

Open the management area and review the membership and invitation status.

🔗
Invite

Select the intended golfer and send their invitation link privately. Check whether the status becomes Invited or Joined.

Do not create duplicate golfer records for an existing person. Review the roster before using an add or re-invite action.

7. Administrator: edit scores and correct mistakes

📚
Locate

Find the golfer's round in History.

✏️
Correct

Use the available Edit action, change only the incorrect fields and save.

🔍
Confirm

Reopen the round and verify the score, course/tee information and recalculated handicap.

Ordinary members may have a limited correction window; administrators can perform corrections allowed by group permissions.

8. Administrator: courses and groups

Review the course list before adding a new course. Choose the correct tees, ratings and slopes. Only edit a course where you have permission. Historical rounds keep their recorded course information. Group owner-only options, such as certain group-level changes, may not appear for administrators.

9. Group Owner: responsibilities

The owner manages ownership-level settings and administrator permissions. Group deletion is a separate, potentially destructive process. Save and verify a backup first and read every confirmation carefully.

10. Backups, offline use and account safety

☁️
Sync

Wait for the app to show that pending work is synced.

💾
Backup

Use the app's backup action and save the file in a location you can find again.

🔐
Protect

Keep your password and personal invitation link private.

11. Delete your account

Find Delete my account at the bottom of the app. Read the explanation and confirm only if you intend to remove your sign-in. Group owners must resolve their owned groups first. Shared golfer names and round history may remain for the group's records; see the Privacy Policy.

12. Help

For problems, record the action and the exact error message and contact willyros01@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or invitation codes.
