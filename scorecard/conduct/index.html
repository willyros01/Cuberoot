The Scorecard Code of Conduct

Effective October 1, 2026

The Scorecard is a shared golf-handicap and scorekeeping app. Golfers in the same group see each other's names, handicaps and, depending on their role, rounds. This code keeps it a fair and pleasant place for everyone. Every member and every group agrees to it when they apply, request a group or accept an invitation.

Zero tolerance. The Scorecard has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Anyone who does not follow this code may be removed, and a group may be closed, at any time.

Be courteous and respectful

  • Treat other golfers the way you would on the course: politely and fairly.
  • Do not harass, threaten, insult or embarrass anyone.
  • Do not discriminate against anyone for who they are.

Use your real name

  • Join under your own name, the one your group knows you by.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else, and do not create records for people who do not exist.
  • Keep one account for yourself. Do not share it.

Enter honest scores

  • Enter the scores that were actually played, on the course and tees actually played.
  • Do not change or invent scores to raise or lower a handicap.
  • If you enter rounds for others, as an admin or organiser, enter them accurately.

Post nothing offensive or abusive

  • Names, group names and notes must not be offensive, obscene, hateful or threatening.
  • Do not use The Scorecard to advertise, spam or collect other people's information.

Respect other people's privacy

  • What you see of other golfers is for your group's golf only. Do not copy or share it elsewhere.
  • Keep invitation links and group codes to the people they were meant for.

Group admins and organisers

  • Invite only golfers who want to take part.
  • Use your rights to run the group fairly, and remove members only for good reason.
  • The Scorecard's owner remains the owner of every group and may step in when needed.

Reporting a problem

In the public group, tap Report or block under a name to report it to the people who run the group, or to hide it from your own screens. In a private group, tell your group's admin. You can always write to willyros01@gmail.com.

What happens when the code is broken

Depending on what happened, The Scorecard may ask for a change, correct or remove content, remove a member from a group, close an account's access, or close a group. Approval of an application or a group request is at The Scorecard's discretion, and it may decline any application without giving a reason.

Changes

If this code changes, this page will show the new effective date.
