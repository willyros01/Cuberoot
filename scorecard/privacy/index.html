The Scorecard Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

The Scorecard is a shared golf-handicap and scorekeeping app. It does not use advertising or advertising tracking.

Information stored

The app stores the information needed to operate your account and golf groups, including:

  • Your email address when you choose email-and-password sign-in.
  • Your Firebase account identifier.
  • Your golfer name, group memberships and role.
  • Rounds, scores, course information, handicap calculations and games entered by group members.
  • Bug-report text and technical details only when you choose to send a report.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Account and golf data are stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services operated by Google Cloud. Members of a group can see the group information the app shows to them. Group owners and administrators have additional access needed to manage their group and correct scores. Cuberoot Systems Architects Ltd. may access stored data only when needed to operate, secure or support the service.

Other services

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: sign-in, account identifiers and app data such as golfer names and scores.
  • Formspree: receives the report text and contact information you deliberately include when you send a bug report.
  • golfcourseapi.com: receives course-search text so the app can find course and tee information.

Like any internet service, these providers may receive routine connection information such as an IP address. Their own privacy terms apply.

Account deletion

In the app, open Settings, choose Delete my account, read the explanation and confirm. A group owner must transfer ownership or delete the group before deleting their own account.

Deletion normally completes immediately. If the app is closed, the device is lost or the network fails during deletion, an automated completion process finishes it within one day.

What remains after deletion

Your sign-in, memberships, invitation claims and link between your account and golfer are removed. Your golfer name, rounds and handicap history remain so the other golfers' shared group history and calculations stay accurate. Old rounds or games may retain a random, unusable account identifier in fields such as enteredBy or createdBy; it no longer leads to an account or personal profile.

Children

The Scorecard is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact
