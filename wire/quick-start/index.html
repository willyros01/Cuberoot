1. Open Wire

Wire combines Philippine and world reporting in a calm, easy-to-read interface.

  1. On an Amazon Fire or Android device, visit the Wire card on Cuberoot and tap Download Android APK. Open the file and follow the device's install prompt.
  2. Launch Wire from its icon. No Wire account or password is required.
  3. On iPhone, iPad or computer, open your existing Wire web-app shortcut or bookmark.

Good to know: Install signed APKs only from Cuberoot.

2. Choose headlines

The opening screen lists stories from enabled publishers.

  1. Tap All sources to see everything, or select a publisher chip such as Inquirer, GMA News, BBC World, or CBC Top Stories.
  2. Scroll through the headlines. Each card shows a publisher label and relative publication time.
  3. The Updated timestamp reports the published feed that Wire loaded; it does not mean every publisher updated at that instant.

3. Read a story and go back

Tap a headline to open Wire's cleaner article reader.

  1. Read the available article text without the original site's embedded advertising.
  2. Tap ← All stories or ← [publisher] headlines to return to your headline list. Wire normally restores your position.
  3. If marked Headline only or Cut short, use Read it on [publisher] when offered to open the publisher's website.

4. Make text larger

The reading tools stay visible across the top of Wire.

  1. Tap A+ to enlarge text. Tap A− to reduce it; seven sizes range from 17 to 50 pixels.
  2. Tap the half-filled circle to turn high contrast on or off.
  3. Tap the moon/sun icon to cycle automatic, day, and night appearance. Choices are saved on this device.

5. Show or hide publishers

The + Sources chip opens your publisher settings.

  1. Tap + Sources after the publisher chips.
  2. Use On or Off beside each source to choose what appears in your headlines.
  3. Tap ← Back to stories to return. Hiding a source does not delete its published content.

6. Enable breaking alerts

News notifications are optional and must be enabled on each device.

  1. On the main list, scroll to Breaking-news notifications below the stories.
  2. Tap Turn on and allow notifications when the device asks.
  3. The same panel offers Turn off. If it shows Repair notifications, tap it once while online.

Good to know: On iPhone or iPad, first add the Wire web app to the Home Screen and open it from its icon. Amazon Fire uses ADM; supported Google Android devices use FCM.

7. Refresh and read offline

Wire checks for newly published stories when connected.

  1. Tap the ↻ top-bar control to check for the latest published feed.
  2. Offline, some previously cached stories may remain available, but fresh news requires the internet.
  3. If a notification arrives before its story appears, reconnect and tap ↻. The publication and notification paths can finish at different times.

8. Quick troubleshooting

Start with simple visible checks before reinstalling.

  1. No headlines? Confirm your connection, tap All sources, then ↻.
  2. One source empty? Try another chip and check + Sources for its On/Off state.
  3. Missing notifications? Check Wire's breaking-news status and the device notification permission.
