1. What Wire does

Wire is a straightforward reader, with accessible controls and optional breaking alerts.

  1. Read recent Philippine and world news in a layout without embedded news-site advertisements.
  2. Select publishers, enlarge text, change contrast and appearance, and opt in to alerts.
  3. Wire needs no personal reader login; publishers remain the sources of the stories.

2. Install or open the app

Wire is available as a web app and a signed Cuberoot Android APK.

  1. Amazon Fire / Android: locate the Wire card on Cuberoot, tap Download Android APK, open the downloaded file, review the installer prompt and launch Wire.
  2. iPhone / iPad: open your Wire web link in Safari. To make a dedicated icon, choose Share → Add to Home Screen; launch that icon to request supported web notifications.
  3. Computer: use your existing Wire bookmark or web app shortcut.
  4. Download only from Cuberoot; installing an Android APK may need permission for the browser that downloaded it.

3. Tour the main screen

The top bar, source chips, story list and notification panel are separate areas.

  1. Top bar: ↻ refresh, A− smaller text, A+ larger text, high contrast, day/night.
  2. Publisher row: All sources, named publishers, and + Sources.
  3. Story list: outlet tag, relative age, headline, summary and a real photo when supplied.
  4. Below the list: Breaking-news notifications and About this app.

4. Filter by publisher

Publisher chips change what is displayed, not what a publisher has written.

  1. Tap All sources to see published stories from enabled outlets, newest first.
  2. Tap a publisher chip to show only its news. Scroll the chip row horizontally if needed.
  3. For an empty filtered list, choose another publisher or inspect its state in + Sources.

5. Read a full article

The reader displays one story at a time.

  1. Tap a headline card; Wire displays the title, outlet, available byline and article text.
  2. Use the reader's ← All stories or ← [publisher] headlines button to return.
  3. Supported Android devices also handle the system Back key. For web, prefer the visible in-app Back button.
  4. Wire normally restores the prior list scroll position.

6. Headline only and Cut short

Some publishers do not expose the entire article to the reader.

  1. Headline only means Wire received feed headline/summary information without full article text.
  2. Cut short means only part of the original story was available.
  3. Use Read it on [publisher] when shown. The original site may include advertisements or subscription requirements.
  4. Wire shows a real supplied image if available; it does not invent a replacement photo.

7. Adjust large-print text

Wire offers seven font settings.

  1. Use A+ or A− to choose 17, 20, 24, 29, 35, 42, or 50 pixels.
  2. At the largest sizes, the layout adjusts to put reading text first.
  3. Your choice is saved on this device.

8. High contrast and day/night

Contrast and appearance are independent preferences.

  1. Tap the half-filled circle for High contrast.
  2. Tap the moon/sun button repeatedly: Automatic → Day → Night → Automatic.
  3. Automatic appearance follows the configured Manila sunrise/sunset, not the device's GPS position.
  4. Explicit Day or Night remains chosen until you change it.

9. Turn publishers on and off

Sources controls which headlines you see.

  1. Open + Sources from the publisher row.
  2. Each item shows its name, feed address, and story count or No stories yet.
  3. Tap On or Off; then ← Back to stories.
  4. Switching off one outlet does not delete its published stories.

10. Remove or restore a publisher

Deleting a source removes your list entry, not the publisher's server data.

  1. Tap Delete next to a source and review the confirmation.
  2. Choose Keep it to cancel or Remove to proceed.
  3. A short-lived Undo option may restore it immediately.
  4. Use Restore the standard list to bring back built-in sources, knowing this may alter custom source selections.

11. Add a new source

Saving an address locally is not the same as enabling server-side collection.

  1. In + Sources, enter a Name and Address, then tap Add source.
  2. The new entry may show No stories yet, even when its switch is On.
  3. A feed must also be in Wire's maintained server-side source list for scheduled fetching to publish its stories.
  4. Where preset additions are offered, they are already known to the fetcher.

Good to know: Adding an arbitrary source in the app does not automatically cause news articles to be collected.

12. Refresh and publication time

The refresh button fetches what Wire has already published.

  1. Tap ↻ to request the latest available feed while online.
  2. The Updated label belongs to the feed; each article has its own age.
  3. News collection, publication, and device alert delivery are separate steps, so their timestamps can differ.
  4. If an alert appears before a story, wait briefly and refresh again.

13. Offline availability

Caching helps with previously loaded content but is not a complete saved-news archive.

  1. Open Wire while online before expecting offline reading.
  2. Previously cached pages or articles might remain accessible, depending on the device and storage.
  3. New headlines and notification registration require internet access.
  4. Clearing browser or app data may remove saved reading preferences and cached content.

14. Enable breaking-news alerts

Alerts are optional and configured separately on each device.

  1. Find Breaking-news notifications beneath the stories on the main screen.
  2. Tap Turn on, then allow notifications at the operating system prompt.
  3. Confirm the status becomes Notifications are on.
  4. Only stories selected by Wire's alert rules generate notifications; ordinary updates may have no alert.

15. Notification differences by device

Web and native devices use different delivery services.

  1. Amazon Fire HD: Wire's installed Android app uses Amazon Device Messaging (ADM).
  2. Supported Google Android devices: the Android app uses FCM.
  3. iPhone/iPad web app: add Wire to the Home Screen and launch that icon before asking for web notifications.
  4. Reader functionality continues even if a browser or device does not support push.

16. Open a notified article

Wire attempts to open the exact story referenced by a notification.

  1. Tap the notification. When its identity is available, Wire navigates to that story.
  2. If needed, Wire can fetch the exact article while online.
  3. A saved web notification route normally expires after 30 minutes; an expired alert may lead to current headlines.
  4. An already-running iPhone web app sometimes receives no tapped-notification identity from iOS; Wire cannot then guarantee opening the specific story.

Good to know: A delivered alert and a news article becoming visible are not necessarily simultaneous.

17. Turn off or repair alerts

Breaking alerts and reading preferences are separate settings.

  1. Use Turn off in Wire's Breaking-news notifications panel on each device where you want alerts stopped.
  2. Tap Repair notifications only if the button appears; keep a working connection during repair.
  3. Check device notification permissions and background restrictions if alerts stop arriving.
  4. Changing notifications on one device does not necessarily change another.

18. Privacy and device settings

The reader has no personal Wire account.

  1. The current web reader keeps source and text preferences locally; its reader-settings synchronization is not configured.
  2. Push registration uses a separate anonymous device identity.
  3. External publisher links open their own sites, which may track browsing or show advertisements.
  4. Clearing local application storage can remove reader settings and offline content.

19. Troubleshooting checklist

Diagnose from the screen before reinstalling or changing device settings.

  1. Nothing in the feed: verify internet, select All sources, then tap ↻.
  2. One publisher missing: inspect + Sources for an Off switch or No stories yet.
  3. Small type: use A+ until comfortable; the button stops at the largest setting.
  4. Partial story: look for Headline only, Cut short or the link to the publisher.
  5. No push: check both Wire's toggle and operating-system permissions; repair only when indicated.
  6. Alert but no story: wait for publishing, reconnect, refresh and inspect current headlines.
